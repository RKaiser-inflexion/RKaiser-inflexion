# Hi, I'm Richard 👋

I build software that companies run on, and I've been building businesses since I was 17.
Today I'm co-founder & CTO of [Evaluro](https://evaluro.inflexion.cz) and I lead [Inflexion](https://www.inflexion.cz). In both, I own the technology: from the first architecture sketch to keeping it running in production.

## What I'm building

- **[Evaluro](https://evaluro.inflexion.cz)**: helps companies screen candidates faster and more fairly. Every role gets a structured interview plan and scoring rubric, candidates complete an AI-led interview, and recruiters get a transparent, evidence-based evaluation. The final decision always stays with a human.
  I designed and built it from scratch: the web app, the AI evaluation engine and the safeguards around it (measurable evaluation quality, prompt-injection defences, responsible AI in line with the EU AI Act).
- **[Inflexion](https://www.inflexion.cz)**: custom software that clients run their business on. We use AI where it genuinely helps, not as the default answer. Currently:
  - **Inflexion Verified**: a verification API and website badge that lets customers confirm in one click that a financial advisor is genuinely certified.
  - **Lead qualification**: finds local businesses, scores how outdated their website is and drafts outreach for a person to review.
  - **Operational systems**: e.g. everything that runs a gym with no front desk (memberships, payments, invoicing, door access).
- **[Šeptem](https://septem.inflexion.cz)**: Czech-first dictation for macOS. Hold fn, speak, and the text lands at your cursor.
- **[Nuppo](https://nuppo.inflexion.cz)**: a small shelf in the MacBook notch for screenshots, downloads and quick notes.
